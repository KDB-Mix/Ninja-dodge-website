Privacy Policy for Ninja Dodge

Last Updated: May 5, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Ninja Dodge handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent gaming experience.

2. Information Collection

Ninja Dodge does not require you to provide personal information such as your name or email address. However, we use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Games Services: Used for leaderboards and achievements. This service may collect your player ID and game progress.
  • Google AdMob: Used for displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect your device’s Advertising ID and usage data to personalize ads. *This is applicable only when advertisements are enabled.*
  • Google Play Billing: Used for processing in-app purchases (e.g., "Remove Ads").

3. In-App Purchases & Payments (Remove Ads)

Ninja Dodge offers a "Remove Ads" in-app purchase. All financial transactions are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or other financial details on our servers. Google provides us only with confirmation of purchase and a transaction ID to provide the service to you.

Effect of "Remove Ads": Once purchased, this will disable all interstitial (pop-up) and banner advertisements within the application. Please note that optional Rewarded Advertisements (ads that the user explicitly chooses to watch in exchange for an in-game reward) may still be available.

4. Permissions

The app may request certain permissions necessary for its core functionality, such as network access to load advertisements (when applicable), update leaderboards, and verify in-app purchase status.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not store your personal data on our own servers. Data related to Google Play Games or purchase history can be managed through your Google account settings or the Google Play Store app.

6. Third-Party Links

The app may contain advertisements (if enabled or if they are rewarded ads) that may lead to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through the developer contact information provided on the Google Play Store.
